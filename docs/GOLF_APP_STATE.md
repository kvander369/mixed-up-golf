### iOS safe areas — the fix that took three goes

`viewport-fit=cover` plus `apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style:black-translucent`
means the web view extends **under** the status bar. Without compensation the
clock and wifi icons land on top of the COURSE / PLAYERS / NEW buttons.

**What works:** the green app bar carries the inset itself and grows upward —

```css
.appbar{
  padding-top: env(safe-area-inset-top);
  height: calc(60px + env(safe-area-inset-top));
}
```

The clock then sits on green and the buttons sit below it.

**What does NOT work — do not repeat it:** putting
`padding-top:env(safe-area-inset-top)` on `.screen`. `.screen` is
`height:100%` inside a `100dvh` stage, so padding there shifts the whole flex
column down, the tab bar swells past the bottom of the viewport, and a black
strip appears beneath it. The top overlap is not fixed either. Tried on
2026-08-23; reverted.

Bottom inset is already handled on `.tabbar` and `.shell` and needs no change.

## Structure as built

**Top of screen = set once, before the round:** `Course` (Pars / Rankings
sub-tabs) · `Players` · `New`.

**Bottom tabs = during and after:** `Holes` · `Scores` · `Results`.

Kyle's principle, 2026-08-23: *"Buttons at the top are pre-round info. Buttons at
the bottom are during and/or after the round use."*

| Screen | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Course** | 18 pars + 18 hole rankings, entered in 9-hole chunks on a 3x3 pad |
| **Players** | 8 names + course handicaps, typed. Team 2 blank = a four-player round |
| **Holes** | Team 1's foursome only. The inside game, live. Tap any hole to enter it |
| **Hole entry** | One hole, one screen: 4 scores on the keypad, junk grid, greenie steppers |
| **Scores** | Player-by-player nine entry — the after-the-round path, and where team 2 goes |
| **Results** | Progress while incomplete; team-vs-team 2-best-ball-net and skins when done |

**Holes and Scores share one store.** Both read and write `state.scores`; there
is no sync step and nothing can drift.

## The two things most likely to be broken by a future tidy-up

### 1. The two games allocate strokes DIFFERENTLY

- **Inside game** (Holes): pops = course handicap **− low man in the foursome**
- **CCW 8-player + random draw**: **raw course handicap**, nothing subtracted

Same formula, different input — which is exactly why sharing one function looks
right and is silently wrong. Guarded by `pops_separation_test.js`.
Full detail in `GOLF_PWA_PART2_RULES.md` §2b.

### 2. Only gross scores reach the screen

Net is computed to colour holes and never rendered per-hole. The one net figure
on screen is the team total on Results, labelled as net. See §4b of the rules doc.

## Players is home plate

Kyle, 2026-08-23: *"Players is in essence the home page. Home plate if you will."*

**Every New Round lands on Players**, whatever option was chosen. The demo is the
only exception — it is already complete, so it opens on Holes.

This is not hand-holding. The roster sets who the low man is; the low man sets
everyone's pops; pops decide every hole colour and every net result. A name in
the wrong slot scores the entire round wrong and **nothing on screen looks
broken**. It has to be right before anything else means anything.

It also genuinely changes every round: the group re-throws balls to remix the
foursomes each time they play, so slot order is never the same twice. Kyle is
always slot 1.

**Slot order is load-bearing:** slots 1–4 are Kyle's foursome, and within it
1&2 play 3&4 in the inside game. Slots 5–8 are team 2; leaving them blank makes
it a four-player round.

**Opening behaviour:** first ever run opens on Players. Every run after that
reopens on the exact screen the app was left on, with the full round restored —
scores, junk, roster, course and the draw. Nothing ever clears itself; only
New Round clears anything.

Kyle, 2026-08-23: *"we should probably keep it for testing and for showing off

complete, so it opens on Holes. The rule is "land on Players whenever the roster
needs attention", and here it does not.

## Decisions made 2026-08-23 that are easy to accidentally undo

- **No holes-won ticker.** Kyle plays 5 ways with automatic presses; a count of
  holes won says nothing about who owes what. Removed on his instruction.
- **No Nassau logic, no presses.** The app colours holes; Kyle does the money.
- **Junk is hand-entered.** No rules to derive.
- **Junk counters show the ROUND TOTAL**, carried across holes; tapping adds one
  booked against the current hole (small green `+n` in the corner). At 18 the
  grid shows the whole round's junk at a glance.
- **Scores above 9 cannot be entered.** Deliberate.
- **Pops markers are never green or gold** — white clipped corner for one shot,
  pink for two. Green/gold mean who won the hole and nothing else.
- **App starts empty.** No roster carries over except via New Round.
- **First-time reassurances stripped.** Kyle: *"if the app works, which it will,
  no need to tell me every letter is saved."*

## OPEN — the live bug

**Kyle reports "New Round › Fill a demo round" does nothing in his browser.**

Status: not reproduced. `golf_app_smoke.js` renders every screen and fires every
control's real handler, all passing. CSS brace balance verified. All
`getElementById` / `querySelector` targets verified present.

Two real bugs of this class were already found and fixed today, both caused by
**over-matching regexes during refactors**:

1. `initials()` deleted in the CSS-Grid rewrite but still called → Holes threw.
2. `renderHoleEntry()` swallowed whole by the Pops-removal regex → tapping a
   hole did nothing.

Lesson recorded: **stop editing this file with broad `perl -0pi` regexes.** Two
functions and nearly 10KB were destroyed that way. Use targeted edits, and run
`node golf_app_smoke.js` after every change.

Latest build adds an on-screen red error bar (`#errbar`) plus `window.onerror`,
so the next failure reports its own message instead of dying silently. **Next
step: have Kyle refresh and read what the bar says.**

Also added: a guard that resets `state.step` to `holes` if a saved round names a
screen that no longer exists — the removed Pops tab was a candidate for exactly
this, and would have produced a blank screen on load.
